## 1. Overview & System Mission

The **AI Business Intelligence Center** is the global cognitive intelligence layer that powers the entire Eviona Ecosystem. Rather than operating as isolated text-generation prompts or generic chat widgets, this layer acts as an autonomous business co-pilot and contextual reasoning engine for every entrepreneur on the platform.

The AI engine understands each user's unique business identity, brand voice, custom domain, product portfolio, target audiences, lead pipelines, and commercial goals to deliver tailored recommendations, continuous optimization, and cross-module automation.

## 2. Core Capabilities & Architecture

### 2.1 Personalized Business Assistants
- **Context-Aware Co-Pilot:** Ingests the entrepreneur's store catalog, active leads, website sections, and financial goals. When asked questions, the assistant produces domain-accurate strategies without requiring the user to re-explain their brand.
- **Brand Voice Synthesis:** Analyzes existing landing page copy and social posts to maintain tone consistency (e.g., professional, energetic, authoritative, casual) across all generated assets.

### 2.2 Custom AI Chatbot Creation (Storefront & Lead Gen Bots)
- **Embedded Conversational Agents:** Entrepreneurs can generate and deploy custom AI chatbots onto their personal landing pages (`username.eviona.com` or custom domains).
- **Knowledge Base Binding:** Chatbots are automatically trained on the entrepreneur's marketplace products, FAQs, pricing tiers, and contact policies.
- **Lead Capture & Routing:** Chatbots qualify incoming visitors in real-time, capture contact details, and push structured leads directly into the entrepreneur's CRM with full conversation transcripts.

### 2.3 Automated Website & Funnel Generation
- **Dynamic Content Synthesis:** Generates complete landing page structures, hero copy, value proposition matrices, FAQ accordions, and CTA buttons tailored to specific industry niches.
- **A/B Variation Engine:** Generates high-converting copy variants for headline testing and funnel optimization.

### 2.4 CRM & Sales Intelligence
- **Predictive Lead Scoring:** Evaluates lead source, engagement velocity, and form metadata to assign real-time conversion probability scores (0–100%).
- **Next-Best-Action Recommendations:** Prompts the entrepreneur with prescriptive follow-up timing and tailored email scripts when leads reach key pipeline stages.

## 4. Business Rules & Governance

1. **Constitutional Transparency (Book 0 §16):** All AI-generated deliverables (ad copy, email sequences, business plans) are presented as editable drafts. The engine never auto-publishes without explicit user confirmation.
2. **Data Privacy & Tenant Isolation:** User prompts, customer interaction transcripts, and proprietary brand data are strictly isolated via tenant security keys and never leaked across user boundaries.
3. **Usage Metering:** AI operations consume metered utility credits from the user's allowance or EVO Token balance, with costs clearly disclosed prior to executing high-compute requests.
