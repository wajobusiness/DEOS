## 1. Executive Summary

DEOS is a subscription-based digital entrepreneur operating system that provides entrepreneurs with an automated dynamic landing page on their own domain, an isolated CRM, email marketing automation, marketplace selling and affiliate rights, AI business tools, and an academy pathway in exchange for a one-time membership fee and an annual renewal. Members earn through their own commercial activity (product/service sales, affiliate promotion) and through a structured, mathematical referral network. The platform earns through membership sales, annual renewals, infrastructure services, marketplace transaction fees, and premium AI/academy content.

The business is built to succeed on **two legs simultaneously**: a real digital-commerce marketplace that generates revenue independent of recruitment, and a referral network that rewards members for growing the community.

## 2. Vision & Mission (Business Framing)

**Vision:** Become the default starting point for anyone in an emerging or global digital economy who wants to launch an online business without first assembling ten separate tools and skills.

**Mission:** Deliver an automated dynamic landing page, a way to sell, a way to learn, and a way to earn — bundled, affordable, and functional from day one of membership.

## 4. Problem & Solution

**Problem:** Launching a digital business today requires separately sourcing and paying for a website builder, domain registrar, hosting, CRM, email autoresponders, e-commerce storefront, course platform, and marketing tools — each with a learning curve, a login, and a bill. Most people give up during setup, before ever reaching a customer.

**Solution:** One membership, one dashboard, one unified multi-tenant platform. The automated Business Launch Wizard provisions a live dynamic landing page, CRM, email sequence automation, and marketplace access in minutes, backed by structured education and a network-based earning path.

## 5. Business Model Overview

DEOS operates a **hybrid multi-tenant subscription + transaction + network model**:

1. **Subscription revenue** — one-time membership fee (100 / 300 / 500 DEOS Coin) + $50/year annual renewal.
2. **Transaction revenue** — marketplace transaction fees on every product/service sale (10% platform fee on promoter sales, 2% on direct sales per Book 5).
3. **Ancillary service revenue** — premium domain registrations, business email, AI credit top-ups, premium masterclasses, enterprise seats.
4. **Member earning layer** — direct referral bonuses ($25/$75/$125), 10% flat binary volume commissions, generation bonuses (30%/15%), and marketplace affiliate commissions (10%–60% + 3% upline overrides).

The Sustainability Fund captures:
- 50% of Split Commission unearned gaps when an under-qualified sponsor refers a higher-tier plan.
- 100% of unqualified Generation Bonuses (Gen 2/Gen 3).
- 100% of inactive sponsor Upline Overrides from marketplace sales.
This fund guarantees operational solvency, server infrastructure scaling, and regulatory reserves.

## 8. Member Journey (End-to-End)

The complete entrepreneur journey is strictly structured:

1. **Discovery & Landing Page:** The prospect arrives on the DEOS public landing page or an active entrepreneur's personal domain (`johnsonagency.com` / `johndoe.deos.com`).
2. **Lead Capture:** Prospect submits contact information into the entrepreneur's CRM with permanent source attribution.
3. **Registration:** Prospect registers for an account; referral code is automatically locked in.
4. **Login & Wallet Auto-Provisioning:** User logs in; a dedicated internal wallet is instantiated.
5. **Wallet Funding:** User deposits funds via USDT (TRC20), Card (Stripe/Paystack), or Bank Transfer.
6. **DEOS Coin Conversion:** Deposit is credited as fixed-value DEOS Coins (Model A: $1.00 USD = 1.00 DEOS).
7. **Membership Purchase:** User selects Launch, Growth, or Legacy plan and checks out in DEOS Coin.
8. **Platform Activation & Binary Placement:** Direct referral bonus and generation waterfall trigger; user is placed in the binary tree via auto-spillover.
9. **Business Environment Provisioning:** Subdomain, dynamic landing page, CRM, email autoresponders, and Academy paths are instantly generated.
10. **Active Entrepreneur Dashboard:** Full access unlocked.
